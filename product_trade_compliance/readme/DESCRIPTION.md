This module enables tracking of trade compliance and export control codes
for products in Odoo. It supports both US Export Administration Regulations
(EAR) ECCN codes and EU Dual-Use Regulation codes.

**Trade Compliance Regimes Supported:**

- **US-EAR**: United States Export Administration Regulations (EAR) - Commerce Control List (CCL)
- **EU-DUAL**: European Union Dual-Use Regulation (EU) 2021/821

**Purpose:**

Export control codes (such as ECCN - Export Control Classification Number)
are regulatory identifiers used to determine whether a product requires a
license for export. Tracking these codes in Odoo enables:

- Compliance with export regulations
- Automated export documentation
- Restriction screening for international shipments
- Regulatory reporting

**Key Features:**

- Manage multiple trade compliance regimes (US, EU, etc.)
- Define compliance codes with descriptions
- Set compliance codes on product categories for automatic inheritance
- Track dual-use and export control codes for individual products
- Search and filter products by trade compliance regime
- View compliance information directly on product forms

## Official Data Sources

The module includes compliance codes from official government sources:

- **EU Dual-Use Regulation**: https://policy.trade.ec.europa.eu/help-exporters-and-importers/exporting-dual-use-items_en
- **US Export Administration Regulations**: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/part-774
