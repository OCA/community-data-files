Print the multimodal dangerous goods form (ADR) of a delivery order.

The report lists every dangerous product of the picking with its UN number,
proper shipping name, class and packing instruction, and sums the transport
points per transport category as defined by ADR 1.1.3.6.3. When the total
exceeds 1,000 points, the exemption of ADR 1.1.3.6 no longer applies and the
report says so.
